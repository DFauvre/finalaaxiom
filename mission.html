Founding Principles & Mission

"Be Your Best Self. Precision Self-Knowledge for Everyone."

I. The Mission Statement

Axiom AI exists to give every person precise, research-backed insight into who they actually are—so they can lead, connect, and decide from their truest self. Not who their parents told them to be, and not who their culture rewarded them for performing.

Built on decades of hands-on research and empirical Enneagram Tritype® methodology developed by David W. Fauvre, M.A., Axiom doesn't guess at your personality; it identifies it accurately and with a level of specificity previously unavailable at scale.

II. Core Operating Principles

  • Precision over comfort: We do not round people off or soften type results. Accuracy is the whole product.
  • Specificity is the point: Built for one person at a time, capturing granular differences that generic wellness advice misses.
  • Human connection is the goal: Axiom is a mirror pointing you back inward, and ultimately toward healthier real-world relationships.
  • Earned authority: Grounded in validated research rather than charismatic stage performance.

III. Founder's Commitment

Having lived with and studied the Enneagram for over 35 years, the breakthrough of modern AI finally makes it possible to deliver individualized insight without multi-million-dollar tech bottlenecks.

"I will personally unplug this if we find it leads to net isolation or degradation of human relationships with self or with others. Period." — David W. Fauvre, M.A.
